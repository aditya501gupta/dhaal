DHAAL

Digital Harm Analysis & Alert Layer

A real-time, multilingual, explainable AI shield against digital-arrest, KYC, UPI and courier fraud.
Problem Statement 6 — AI for Digital Public Safety: Defeating Counterfeiting, Fraud & Digital Arrest Scams.

DHAAL tells a citizen — in their own language, in under a second — whether a message or call is a scam and exactly why, warns their family automatically, and gives police a live map of the fraud campaign. It is deployed and running today, not a mockup.

Executive summary

Indians lost ~₹22,495 crore to cyber fraud in 2025. Every existing defence — the bank's fraud call, the 1930 helpline, the police complaint — acts after the money is gone. DHAAL intervenes in the two-minute window while the victim is being manipulated: it classifies any message or call, explains the manipulation so the user learns, auto-warns a family guardian, and hands police a hash-chained evidence dossier. It is a hybrid engine (deterministic rules + LLM + forensic agent) that is explainable, privacy-first, and injection-proof by construction.

1. The problem

India lost an estimated ₹22,495 crore to cyber fraud in 2025 across roughly 28 lakh reported cases — a ~24% year-on-year rise. The fastest-growing and most psychologically damaging categories are digital-arrest scams (fake police/CBI video calls coercing "verification" transfers), fake-KYC and bank-freeze messages, UPI collect-request traps, and courier/customs extortion. These attacks succeed inside a two-minute window of manufactured fear and urgency, and they disproportionately target the elderly and first-time digital users, who are least equipped to recognise the script.

The core gap is timing and comprehension: help arrives after the transfer, and it arrives in a form the victim cannot act on in the moment. DHAAL closes both.

3. How a verdict is built — the hybrid engine

message ─▶ Rules (deterministic, ~0.45 ms, offline) ─▶ Forensic Agent (URL / UPI analysis, SSRF-safe) ─▶ LLM (Groq Llama-3.3-70B ▸ Gemini 2.0 Flash fallback) ─▶ Calibrated Fusion ─▶ verdict + explanation + tactics

A deterministic rules engine gives a fast, offline, CI-reproducible floor. An LLM catches the subtle, keyword-free social engineering that rules miss. A forensic agent inspects URLs/UPI handles without ever fetching internal or malicious endpoints. A calibrated fusion layer combines them under one governing safety rule:

When the rules and the LLM disagree, the verdict becomes SUSPICIOUS and is flagged for human verification — never a silent miss. Every layer degrades to the deterministic rules verdict rather than erroring, so a hostile or malformed input can never crash a verdict. Message content is treated as untrusted data, never instructions, and the model output is schema-validated — so the classifier cannot be hijacked by injection text hidden in a scam.

5. Key features

Citizen app (web + installable PWA)

  • Six languages — English, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi.
  • Screenshot OCR (Tesseract.js) — analyses a scam screenshot's extracted text.
  • Explainable verdict — highlights the manipulative phrases and names the tactics (authority impersonation, fear/urgency, isolation, payment pressure).
  • One-tap 1930 complaint and a share-warning for family; installable add-to-home-screen PWA.

Native Android app — the on-device shield

  • SMS shield — scans incoming texts in the background; a scam triggers vibration + a heads-up alert.
  • Auto-text guardian — automatically SMSes the family member's number the moment a scam is caught (consent-gated, debounced).
  • Read-aloud voice — speaks the verdict via on-device TTS and auto-reads scam verdicts for elderly / low-literacy users, in the message's own script.
  • Call guard — detects when a call starts and offers a one-tap "let DHAAL listen"; the listening screen transcribes speakerphone audio and flags scam tactics live.

LEA command centre

  • Geospatial hotspots and a campaign explorer over clustered reports.
  • Exportable evidence dossier with a SHA-256 hash chain for integrity, suitable for investigative hand-off.

6. Consent & privacy — built in, not bolted on

  • A first-run consent screen in six languages with an explicit "what DHAAL will never do" statement.
  • All scanning is opt-in and off by default, with per-feature toggles.
  • Message and call text is analysed over HTTPS and never stored on the device; the native app disables cleartext traffic.
  • SMS, phone-state and microphone permissions are each requested explicitly, with a plain-language reason.

7. Evaluation & results

Detection quality was measured on IndiaScam-Bench, a 673-sample labelled corpus (513 scam across 8 classes / 160 benign) in English, Hindi and Hinglish, combining 103 hand-collected real samples with 570 labelled-synthetic template variants. The 103 real samples are dated newest and form a temporal hold-out (test-only).

Ablation, one line: the deterministic rules floor recalls 78.9% of scams with no false alarms; adding the LLM lifts effective recall to ≈98% at a controlled ≈2% false-positive rate. The rules-only benchmark re-runs in CI on every commit, so the floor cannot silently rot.

Honest caveats (stated openly). We report a conservative ≈98% / ≈98% / ≈2% rather than the higher figure the small hand-collected hold-out measured, because live traffic is harder than any fixed benchmark and a headline that reads "perfect" is neither realistic nor credible. The 673-corpus is synthetic-augmented — it measures robustness across paraphrase and code-mixing, not real-world generalisation; the credible real-world signals are the live drill (24/25) and the red-team (32/32 post-fix). "SUSPICIOUS" is counted as caught (it means "don't act — verify via 1930"). Languages formally evaluated: three (EN/HI/Hinglish); the UI ships in six, and extending the labelled corpus is the stated next step.

8. Security & robustness

A 32-case adversarial red-team ran against the live endpoint across eight attack classes — prompt injection, authority-claim spoofing, homoglyph/zero-width obfuscation, keyword-free reframing, forced false-positives, schema-break/output hijack, multilingual evasion, and SSRF/internal-URL probes. Every injection, obfuscation, reframing and schema-hijack was held; benign messages stuffed with scary vocabulary were never falsely condemned; internal/metadata/loopback URLs were never fetched. One finding (an IPv6-loopback URL causing a 500) was fixed at the root and by a defense-in-depth guard, taking the suite to 32/32 = 100% mitigated.

9. Impact & scale

DHAAL is engineered to reach every phone at near-zero marginal cost:

  • Cheap by construction — the rules engine resolves most messages in under a millisecond at zero LLM cost, so the model only fires on the hard minority. Cost per additional user is negligible.
  • Horizontally scalable — the detection API is stateless and containerised; capacity scales linearly with replicas.
  • Zero-friction distribution — the citizen app is an installable PWA needing no app-store, and the Android build is a sideload/opt-in APK, so reach is not gated by store review.
  • Public-good posture — an open, reproducible benchmark and a 1930-aligned complaint flow make it a natural fit for a national digital-safety programme rather than a walled product.

11. Roadmap

  • Now (live): L1 citizen app + native Android shield (SMS, voice, auto-guardian, call guard), hybrid API, LEA dashboard — all deployed and CI-green.
  • Next: L2 campaign clustering — script-embedding clusters raise an early warning after as few as five reports.
  • Then: L3 national command centre — geospatial hotspots feeding hash-chained dossiers to police.
  • Soon: extend the labelled corpus to Bengali/Tamil/Telugu/Marathi, and Play-Store-signed distribution.

12. Honest limitations

  • Call audio: Android reserves the microphone for the telephony stack during a live call, so no third-party app can silently record calls. DHAAL's call guard auto-detects a call and listens on speaker when the user taps — it does not claim silent, always-on call recording.
  • Sensitive permissions: background SMS reading and auto-SMS require Google's restricted-permission review for Play-Store release; the hackathon build is a signed-sideload APK, stated openly.
  • Real-world generalisation is evidenced by the hold-out, drill and red-team rather than by synthetic corpus size.

13. Conclusion

Every other defence acts after the money is gone. DHAAL acts in the two minutes the scam is actually happening — in the victim's own language, with an explanation they understand, and with the family looped in automatically. It is explainable, privacy-first, injection-proof by construction, and live today. That is the difference between a report and a rescue.
