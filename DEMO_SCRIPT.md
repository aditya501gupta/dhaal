# DHAAL — Demo video shot list (target 2:30–3:00)

Record on a phone + screen capture. Keep it fast; judges watch dozens. Each shot has a
spoken line and the on-screen action. Total ~10 shots.

## Shot 0 — Hook (0:00–0:15)
**Say:** "Every day Indians lose crores to digital-arrest, fake-KYC and UPI scams. By the
time you realise, the money's gone. DHAAL is a real-time AI shield that catches it *before*
you tap."
**Show:** DHAAL citizen app open on a phone, logo, tagline.

## Shot 1 — The scam, caught (0:15–0:45)
**Say:** "Here's a real digital-arrest message." Paste it.
**Show:** Paste a scam SMS into the citizen app → verdict **SCAM 0.9x** appears in under a
second, with the plain-language reason and the **1930** helpline button.

## Shot 2 — In your language (0:45–1:00)
**Say:** "The same verdict, in the language a first-time user actually reads."
**Show:** Switch language to Hindi (and one more), re-show the verdict + reason.

## Shot 3 — Screenshot / OCR (1:00–1:15)
**Say:** "Got it as a screenshot? DHAAL reads the image."
**Show:** Upload a scam screenshot → OCR extracts text → SCAM verdict.

## Shot 4 — Elderly + guardian (1:15–1:45)
**Say:** "For an elderly parent, turn on easy-view mode and set a family guardian."
**Show:** Settings → XL mode toggle; set guardian name + number → on the next SCAM verdict
the phone **vibrates** and a one-tap **"Warn guardian"** WhatsApp message pops up.

## Shot 5 — Native app / live SMS (1:45–2:10)
**Say:** "Installed as an app, DHAAL watches incoming SMS in the background — with consent."
**Show:** Install `DHAAL.apk`; receive a scam SMS → **system notification + strong vibrate**
fires automatically; tap "Warn guardian." (If SMS can't be staged, show the consent screen
and the toggle instead and say this runs on the device.)

## Shot 6 — Police command center (2:10–2:30)
**Say:** "Police don't see one case — they see the campaign."
**Show:** dhaal-command.vercel.app dark map: clusters, filters, **Export dossier** with the
SHA-256 hash chain.

## Shot 7 — Under the hood (2:30–2:45)
**Say:** "Rules for speed, an LLM for the subtle ones, and when they disagree we flag for a
human — never a silent miss. It's injection-proof and fails safe."
**Show:** One architecture slide (deck slide) or the SUBMISSION.md diagram.

## Shot 8 — Proof (2:45–2:55)
**Say:** "100% recall on our real hold-out, 24 of 25 on a live drill, and it survived a
32-case red-team."
**Show:** The metrics table from `benchmark_v3_frozen.md`.

## Shot 9 — Close (2:55–3:00)
**Say:** "DHAAL — an AI shield for digital public safety. Live now."
**Show:** The three live URLs on screen.

---

### Recording tips
- Do Shots 1–4 in one continuous phone screen-recording if you can — it reads as "real".
- Pre-load the scam texts in a notes app to paste quickly.
- Keep spoken lines tight; cut dead air. Add captions (many judges watch muted).
